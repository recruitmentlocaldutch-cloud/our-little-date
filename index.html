PRIVATE LETTER // DO NOT RUSH

Dear you,

I don't know exactly what our date will look like. Maybe the weather will cooperate. Maybe it won't. Maybe we'll follow the plan. Maybe we'll forget it completely.

And honestly, I think I'd be happy either way.

Because the part I'm looking forward to isn't the restaurant, the coffee, the lights, the music or even the place.

It's the moment when all of those things become part of a memory that belongs to both of us.

I want to hear the stories you haven't told me yet. I want to discover the little things that make you smile. I want to know what makes you feel safe, what makes you laugh, what makes you dream.

I don't want to rush any of it.

I just want to sit across from you, look at you, and think...

"I'm really glad we finally did this."
