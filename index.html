CHAPTER 02 — THE LITTLE THINGS

I don't need
a perfect date.

I don't need an expensive restaurant, a perfect sunset, or some impossible movie scene.

I just want a few hours where the rest of the world feels quiet.

A table somewhere. Two drinks. A conversation that somehow lasts longer than we expected.

CHAPTER 03 — THE ARRIVAL

The moment
you arrive.

I already know what will happen.

I'll probably pretend I'm completely calm. I'll probably check the time even though I already know exactly what time it is.

And then I'll see you.

And suddenly, every sentence I prepared will disappear.
CHAPTER 04 — SOMEWHERE BETWEEN

Somewhere
between hello
and goodbye.

Maybe we'll talk about everything. Maybe we'll talk about nothing.

Maybe there will be awkward silences. Maybe we'll laugh at things that aren't even funny.

And honestly... I think I'll like those moments too.

CHAPTER 05 — THE CLOCK

I already know
my least favourite moment.

When the clock tells us that it's time to leave.

Because good moments have this unfair habit of ending exactly when you start wishing they wouldn't.

CHAPTER 06 — IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Something probably will.

The weather could change. The restaurant could be crowded. We could get lost. We could miss a train.

But maybe those are the memories we'll talk about years later.

Perfect plans make beautiful photographs. Imperfect days make stories.

CHAPTER 07 — THINGS I HOPE YOU NOTICE

If you look closely...

You'll probably notice that I look at you when you're not looking at me.

You'll notice that I remember small things you probably thought I forgot.

You'll notice that I listen.

Because when someone matters, the little things stop being little.
CHAPTER 08 — THE QUIET SCENE

Maybe my favourite
part is the quiet.

No cameras. No messages. No notifications. No one asking where we are.

Just sitting somewhere, watching the world move around us.

And for a little while, not needing anything else.

CHAPTER 09 — PRIVATE LETTER

Dear you,


I don't know exactly what our date will look like.

Maybe we'll laugh all evening. Maybe we'll be shy at first. Maybe we'll discover that we're both terrible at choosing what to eat.

I don't know.

And strangely, that's what makes it beautiful.

Because I don't want a memory that feels rehearsed.

I want something real.

Something we can look back on and say, "Remember that day?"

And then smile before either of us even finishes the sentence.

That's all I'm asking for.

One day. One chance. One little memory that belongs only to us.

Until then, I'll keep the invitation open.

— Aviyan ❤️

Maybe one day...

Maybe one day we'll come back to this page and laugh at how much thought went into planning one little date.

Maybe we'll remember the exact day. The exact place. The exact weather.

But more than anything, I hope we remember the feeling.

I don't want to write our story for you.
